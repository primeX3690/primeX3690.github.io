The Problem

Chip design still runs on manual iteration

01

Dozens of manual passes

Writing Verilog, synthesizing, then hand-tweaking for area and timing takes dozens of iterations per chip block.

02

Needs senior expertise

Effective RTL optimization requires years of specialist knowledge — it doesn't scale with the number of designs a team needs to ship.

03

Doesn't scale with demand

As chip design demand grows across AI hardware, edge devices, and custom silicon, the manual bottleneck gets worse, not better.

The Solution

RTL that evolves itself, verified by real EDA tools

Genetic-programming optimization

A population of design variants improves generation-by-generation — no manual tweaking required.

Real tools, not a learned approximation

Verilator checks functional correctness and Yosys measures area/timing — the same tools chip engineers already trust.

Every design is bit-exact verified

Correctness is confirmed before area or delay is ever considered, so optimization never trades away functional accuracy.

Live visibility into the process

A dashboard tracks fitness and population diversity generation-by-generation, so engineers can see and steer the optimization.

Business Model

A tool chip teams plug into their existing flow

Phase 1 — Tool licensing

License EvoHDL as a drop-in optimization stage for chip design teams and EDA-adjacent workflows — it works alongside existing Verilator/Yosys-based flows rather than replacing them.

Phase 2 — Integration

Partner with semiconductor design houses and EDA tool vendors to embed EvoHDL deeper into the RTL sign-off pipeline, moving toward recurring per-seat or per-project licensing.

Phase 3 — Platform expansion

Extend the fitness-function approach beyond RTL into broader chip design automation — a foundation for a wider design-automation product line.
